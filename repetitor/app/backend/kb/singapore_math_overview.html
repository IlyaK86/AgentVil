(Quick Definition) Singapore Math is a world-renowned curriculum focused on teaching fewer concepts in greater depth, ensuring students achieve true mastery rather than just memorizing formulas. Developed in the 1980s for Singapore's schools, it consistently propels students to the top of international math rankings. * It relies heavily on the [**CPA method**](https://www.aceacademy.com/en/guides/list/top-singapore-math-online-tutors-north-america) to transition students from physical objects to abstract numbers. * The curriculum emphasizes problem-solving through visual bar modeling techniques. * It focuses on algebraic thinking early on, preparing students for advanced math tracks. * Lessons are structured to build deep mathematical logic rather than rote memorization. → _Read the full Singapore Math explainer_ ## Why Singapore Math Matters in 2026 91% **Stronger logic in 8 lessons:** 91% of families reported stronger logic and problem-solving skills after only 8 lessons of structured Singapore Math. #1 **Global Competitiveness:** In international assessments like TIMSS and PISA, Singapore consistently ranks #1, proving the efficacy of this deep-dive approach. 40% **Bridging the COVID Gap:** With 40% of US 8th graders scoring below basic math levels, Singapore Math provides the rigorous foundation needed to recover lost learning. Singapore Math is a highly focused curriculum that teaches mathematical concepts in depth using a three-step learning process: Concrete (hands-on), Pictorial (visual), and Abstract (numbers and symbols). This ensures students master the "why" behind math, not just the "how." _Read more about the definition_ How does the CPA method work? It starts with physical objects (concrete), moves to drawing diagrams like bar models (pictorial), and finishes with mathematical symbols (abstract). This progression builds a solid bridge to complex algebraic thinking. _See the CPA breakdown_ Is Singapore Math too advanced for my child? While rigorous, Singapore Math is designed to be highly accessible because it builds concepts step-by-step. It is excellent for both struggling students who need clear visuals and advanced students seeking enrichment. _Check our readiness guide_ Does it align with Common Core standards? Yes, Singapore Math heavily influenced the development of the Common Core State Standards, particularly its focus on deep conceptual understanding and mathematical modeling. _Learn about standards alignment_ How can I find qualified Singapore Math online tutors? Look for programs like ACE Academy that employ credentialed teachers with specific training in the CPA method and advanced math tracks. _View tutor selection criteria_ What age is best to start Singapore Math? Children can start as early as age 3 with early childhood programs, but the curriculum is highly effective for any student in the K-8 range. _See age-by-age recommendations_ This comprehensive hub has explored the core pillars of Singapore Math, from the foundational CPA method to advanced bar modeling and curriculum alignment.
